The author

Most crime fiction is imagined. His was lived.

Joe joined the East Providence Police Department in 1978 and spent the next twenty years working its streets and its cases.

He commanded the Investigative Services Division and served on the Organized Crime Drug Enforcement Task Force alongside the FBI and DEA. He retired in 1998 as a captain. That is why his procedure is right, and why the moral lines in his books blur the way they do in real cases.

He always wrote, sending pieces to newspapers and magazines while still on the job. After retiring, and with a push from his wife, Susan, he started writing books in earnest.

Off the job

He grew up in Cumberland, Rhode Island, where he and his friends dug for diamonds on Diamond Hill. That hill became the setting for The Last Dragon. He is a Cumberland High School graduate, a grandfather (the picture books are for his grandchildren), and his short stories appear in the anthologies Run and Scream.

Short bio, for the press

Joe Broadmeadow retired as a captain from the East Providence, Rhode Island Police Department after twenty years, where he commanded the Investigative Services Division and served on the Organized Crime Drug Enforcement Task Force. He writes the Josh Williams and Hawk Bennett crime novels, true crime, a young adult fantasy trilogy and picture books, and founded JEBWizard Creative Media in 2019.
